# Super Arts — Cuphead Wiki (wiki.gg)
Source: https://cuphead.wiki.gg/wiki/Super_Arts

Super Arts are extremely powerful attacks unlocked one by one upon rescuing the Legendary Chalice from each Mausoleum. Activated with a full super meter (5 cards, by parrying or damaging enemies).

| Name | Description | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Energy Beam | A devastating attack spills from your head. Horizontal only (ground or air). | Super Art I |
| Invincibility | Cross the astral plane to become invulnerable for a short time. | Super Art II |
| Giant Ghost | Maneuver your spirit and body simultaneously for maximum damage. | Super Art III |
| Bomb Morph | Transform into a giant bomb that explodes on impact or timeout; 3 s invulnerable after. | Aeroplane |
| Spiral Pillar (Ms. Chalice) | A vertical version of the Energy Beam. | Super Art I |
| Shield Pal (Ms. Chalice) | Summons a familiar that takes one extra hit. | Super Art II |
| Ghostly Barrage (Ms. Chalice) | Unleashes Ms. Chalice's ancient form and launches a spectral volley. | Super Art III |
| Missile Morph (Ms. Chalice) | Giant missile flying forward; steer up/down; 3 s invulnerable after. | Aeroplane |

Trivia: can parry during Super I; Super II doubles as a double jump; holding lock during Super III keeps the body still while steering the ghost; name likely inspired by Street Fighter; Supers ignore heart-charm damage reduction.
