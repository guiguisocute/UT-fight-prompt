**Elder Kettle** is the overarching protagonist of
*[Cuphead](/wiki/Cuphead_(game) "Cuphead (game)")* who acts as the
caretaker of [Cuphead](/wiki/Cuphead_(character) "Cuphead (character)")
and [Mugman](/wiki/Mugman "Mugman"). He is responsible for the brothers'
finger gun powers and possibly writing the
[tutorial](/wiki/The_Tutorial "The Tutorial").

He is voiced by Joe Hanna in *[The Cuphead
Show](/wiki/The_Cuphead_Show "The Cuphead Show")*.

## Description\[[![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBjbGFzcz0id2RzLWljb24gd2RzLWljb24tdGlueSIgYXJpYS1oaWRkZW49InRydWUiIGZvY3VzYWJsZT0iZmFsc2UiPjx1c2UgeGxpbms6aHJlZj0iI3dkcy1pY29ucy1wZW5jaWwtdGlueSIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcuphead.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElder_Kettle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

### Appearance\[[![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBjbGFzcz0id2RzLWljb24gd2RzLWljb24tdGlueSIgYXJpYS1oaWRkZW49InRydWUiIGZvY3VzYWJsZT0iZmFsc2UiPjx1c2UgeGxpbms6aHJlZj0iI3dkcy1pY29ucy1wZW5jaWwtdGlueSIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcuphead.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElder_Kettle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Elder Kettle is a tannish-looking tea kettle with a light fawn lid and
stripe on the bottom of his body and a long spout above his metal
mustache that has a small chip to the side. He wears white gloves, light
fawn shoes and he's always carrying a brown cane.

In *[The Cuphead Show!](/wiki/The_Cuphead_Show! "The Cuphead Show!")* he
looks almost the same, but there are some noticeable differences: he now
wears a small pair of glasses (which he is blind without), a pair of red
slippers and his cane as more of a derby design. Additionally, his color
scheme is more of a grey shade instead of tan, with his lid and stripe
being slate grey.

### Personality\[[![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBjbGFzcz0id2RzLWljb24gd2RzLWljb24tdGlueSIgYXJpYS1oaWRkZW49InRydWUiIGZvY3VzYWJsZT0iZmFsc2UiPjx1c2UgeGxpbms6aHJlZj0iI3dkcy1pY29ucy1wZW5jaWwtdGlueSIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcuphead.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElder_Kettle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Elder Kettle is a classic parental figure, being very wise and always
wanting the brothers to do the right thing.

## Story\[[![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBjbGFzcz0id2RzLWljb24gd2RzLWljb24tdGlueSIgYXJpYS1oaWRkZW49InRydWUiIGZvY3VzYWJsZT0iZmFsc2UiPjx1c2UgeGxpbms6aHJlZj0iI3dkcy1pY29ucy1wZW5jaWwtdGlueSIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcuphead.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElder_Kettle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

Elder Kettle acts as the parental guardian of Cuphead and Mugman,
keeping them out of trouble and warning them constantly not to go to the
casino in [Inkwell Hell](/wiki/Inkwell_Hell "Inkwell Hell"). One day,
the brothers manage to wander far away from home and become in debt to
[The Devil](/wiki/The_Devil "The Devil").

Despite being disappointed by what the brothers have gotten themselves
into, Elder Kettle agrees to help them, for he fears what The Devil will
do to them if they don't succeed. He gives the pair a potion that grants
them the ability to use their [weapons](/wiki/Weapons "Weapons") and
requests that they do the
[tutorial](/wiki/Home_Sweet_Home "Home Sweet Home") before starting
their quest. After Cuphead and Mugman defeat all of the debtors in
[Inkwell Isle One](/wiki/Inkwell_Isle_One "Inkwell Isle One"), Elder
Kettle catches up to them. He reveals that the brothers are growing in
strength and may be able to turn the tables on both [King
Dice](/wiki/King_Dice "King Dice") and maybe even The Devil himself.
However, he also says that they will have to hold onto their contracts
in order to stand a chance against him.

In the good [ending](/wiki/Endings "Endings"), Elder Kettle is seen
participating in the celebration with the other debtors, being proud of
Cuphead and Mugman for their victory against The Devil.

## Trivia\[[![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBjbGFzcz0id2RzLWljb24gd2RzLWljb24tdGlueSIgYXJpYS1oaWRkZW49InRydWUiIGZvY3VzYWJsZT0iZmFsc2UiPjx1c2UgeGxpbms6aHJlZj0iI3dkcy1pY29ucy1wZW5jaWwtdGlueSIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcuphead.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FElder_Kettle%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

- His design may based on the character [Mr.
  Coffeepot](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/24i689oWkjY/hqdefault.jpg) from the
  1935 Van Beuren cartoon *Picnic Panic*. Both characters even have
  cup-like children that they care for.
- When he was younger, Elder Kettle had a more fanciful mustache. He
  even wishes for this mustache as shown in [Woeful Wishes of Cuphead:
  Comic Capers &
  Curios](/wiki/Cuphead:_Comic_Capers_%26_Curios "Cuphead: Comic Capers & Curios").
- Elder Kettle is not a good yodeler. According to Cuphead in [A
  Mountain of
  Trouble](/wiki/Cuphead_in_A_Mountain_of_Trouble:_A_Cuphead_Novel "Cuphead in A Mountain of Trouble: A Cuphead Novel"),
  it sounds like "someone was badly mistreating a goose".
- Statues of somebody bearing a close resemblance to Elder Kettle can be
  seen in the background of [Rugged
  Ridge](/wiki/Rugged_Ridge "Rugged Ridge"), maybe suggesting a
  connection between him and [The Legendary
  Chalice](/wiki/The_Legendary_Chalice "The Legendary Chalice"), since
  her statue is also seen in the same place.
